Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website.

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (opens in new tab) (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. They help websites work well for people who use a screen reader or a keyboard, and we fix problems when we find them.

What we already do

  • Photos have short text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Every page starts with a “Skip to main content” link and uses headings in order, so you can move around without a mouse.
  • The menus and buttons work with a keyboard, and a colored outline shows where you are.
  • Pages fit small and large screens, and you can zoom in on the text.
  • Links that open in a new tab say so.

What we’re still working on

  • Our sign-up forms are made by Jotform, and we have its accessibility setting turned on for all of them. We can’t control everything about how they work, so if a form gives you trouble, email us and we’ll help you sign up another way.
  • Our one-page 2026 to 2027 calendar (opens in new tab) is a PDF, and some screen readers may not read it well. The important dates are also in our FAQ. For any others, email us and we’ll send them another way.

Tell us if something doesn’t work

If anything on this site is hard to use, email us at hello@littleroots.school. Tell us which page it was and what happened, and we’ll fix it or find another way to get you the information.
