Enroll in Little Roots

We’re so glad you’re here! Here’s how to join us at Little Roots.

We meet every Wednesday from 9:30 to 11 AM at McCollum Park in Everett, rain or shine. It’s a small group of up to 15 families with kids ages 1 to 3 (three year olds are welcome until they turn 4), and a parent or caregiver stays for the whole class.

A few spots are open right now, and you can join anytime!

Already know you’re joining? Jump to the enrollment form. (opens in new tab)

Start With a Trial Class

Most families start by trying one morning with us for $15. If you enroll after, the rest of that month is $24 for each class left, up to $74. Please book by 3 PM Tuesday so I can email you the meeting spot in time.

After You Enroll

  1. 1

    Check your email for the waiver

    Jotform sends your confirmation and our liability waiver right away. Please sign the waiver before your first class.

  2. 2

    Watch for your welcome email

    Within a day or two, you’ll get the meeting spot and parking details.

    Don’t see it? Check your spam folder and add hello@littleroots.school to your contacts.

  3. 3

    Pack your bag

    Our Gear Guide has everything you’ll need, plus family discounts like 25% off OAKI orders over $29.99. Come ready to get muddy.

    Joining in the colder months? See Cold and Wet Weather for how we set up class, plus tips to keep your family comfortable.
