Register for Little Roots

We’re so glad you’re here! Registration is quick and easy - just a few steps and you’re in.

Founding Families: Every family who enrolls in our June 2026 session pays no registration fee. It’s our thank you for believing in Little Roots from the start. Questions? Learn more in our FAQ or email us.

Before registering, please read our Family Handbook.

Spots are limited to 15 families per session.

  1. 1

    Fill out the registration form below

    This tells us about your family so we can welcome you properly.

  2. 2

    Complete your payment through Stripe

    You’ll be redirected to our secure payment page after submitting the form.

  3. 3

    You’re in!

    You’ll receive a confirmation and welcome email within 24 hours.

Select the payment option that matches what you chose on the registration form.

Monthly - $99/month Full Year Bundle - $999 (June–May) School Year Bundle - $799 (Sept–May)

Bundles waive the $49 registration fee.
