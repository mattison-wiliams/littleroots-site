Tutoring with Mattison

I’m a Washington-licensed K-8 teacher offering one-on-one tutoring for elementary and middle school students, online or in person around Everett.

About me

I taught 7th and 8th grade science for three years. Before that, I taught preschool at A Little Darling School in Bellingham under my mentor, Netta Darling, while earning my degree in Elementary Education from Western Washington University. Today I run Little Roots Forest School, a nature-based class for toddlers and their grown-ups. I’m also a mom, and I’m CPR and First Aid certified.

What I tutor

  • Elementary (K-5): any subject, including reading, writing, and math.
  • Middle school (6-8): science, language arts, and history.
  • Study skills and organization, like keeping a planner and breaking big assignments into smaller steps.

Why fall is a good time to start

First report cards and parent-teacher conferences often show where a child is having a hard time. Starting in the fall gives you the rest of the school year to work on it.

How it works

Sessions are one-on-one. We can meet online, at your home, or at a library, whatever is easiest for your family. In-person sessions are available within about 15 minutes of Everett. Let me know which days and times work for you and we’ll find a time.

I’m also happy to work with two kids from the same family together if they’re at similar levels, or a small group of friends. Another option is two kids back to back, 30 minutes each. Tell me what you have in mind and we’ll figure out what works for your kids.

Payment and cancellations

Payment is due before each session through a secure Stripe link I’ll send you. If you need to cancel, please let me know at least 24 hours ahead. Sessions canceled with less notice are charged in full.

If your child is sick, just let me know and we’ll reschedule at no charge. If I ever need to cancel, you won’t be charged and we’ll find a new time.
