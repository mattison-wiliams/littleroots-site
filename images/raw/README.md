# Photo uploads (originals go here)

Drop your **original, full-size session photos** into the subfolders below.
Don't worry about resizing, renaming, or compressing — Claude will optimize
each photo (web-sized JPG/WebP), write alt text, and wire it into the pages.

After photos are placed on the site, this `raw/` folder can be deleted so the
large originals don't stay in the repo.

## Where to put each photo

| Subfolder    | What goes here                                                        | Where it ends up on the site |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| `morning/`   | Candid shots of a session in progress — circle time, nature walk, snack | Homepage "What a Morning Looks Like" timeline |
| `stations/`  | The stations: mud kitchen, sensory play, art, nature exploration, motor skills | Approach page "At the Stations" |
| `intro/`     | One strong wide shot of a session / kids + grown-ups in the forest    | Homepage "What Is Little Roots?" |
| `crowns/`    | Finished crowns, or kids wearing them                                 | Approach page "Crown Celebration" |
| `gallery/`   | Any other nice candids you want shown                                 | "Moments from the Forest" photo grid |

Not sure where one goes? Just drop it in `gallery/` and Claude will sort it.

## Two notes
- **Consent:** only include children whose families are OK with their photo
  being published publicly. For kids who can't be shown, prefer hands / wide /
  back-of-head shots.
- **Filenames don't matter** — phone names like `IMG_4724.jpeg` are fine.
